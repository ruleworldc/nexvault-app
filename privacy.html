Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026 · Operator: NexVaultApp ("NexVault", "we", "us") · Applies globally

This explains what NexVault stores about you, why, and who else sees it. We keep it short because we keep little.

1. What we store

Telegram account: your Telegram user ID, and your username and first name if Telegram shares them. This is your login; there is no password and no email sign-up.

Wallet addresses: the public addresses you connect or type as a recipient. These are public blockchain identifiers, not keys.

Swap records: for each swap started through NexVault: the chains and tokens, amounts, the wallet app used, the transaction hash, and the sending and receiving addresses. We do not store your private keys, seed phrase or wallet contents.

Games and NV: your NV and gem balances, entries, scores, winnings, purchases, cash-out requests, check-ins, referrals and tier status.

Approximate location: the country and region derived from your IP address, used to apply country rules for games and for fraud prevention. The IP-to-country result is cached for up to 30 days. We do not store precise location.

Support messages: what you write in the Support tab and any contact detail you choose to add.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not hold funds, keys or seed phrases.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. The app keeps a few settings on your device (such as debug flags and session state) so it works between visits.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the service: show balances, match payments, pay winnings, process cash-outs, credit referrals.
  • To keep records of swaps and payments so we can investigate problems you report and reconcile our fees.
  • To apply country rules for games and to detect abuse (duplicate accounts, reward farming).
  • To answer support requests.

4. Who else sees it

To do a swap, your wallet address and the swap details are sent to the services that quote and route it (currently Li.Fi for EVM, Solana, Bitcoin and Tron routes; DeDust, STON.fi and Omniston for TON; Jupiter as a fallback on Solana) and to blockchain nodes and price feeds (such as TonCenter, Helius, DexScreener and exchange price APIs). Your wallet app and WalletConnect see what they need to connect and sign. Telegram sees that you opened the Mini App. Our data is hosted on Supabase; support emails are sent through Resend. Each of these has its own privacy policy. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Blockchain transactions are public by nature. Anyone can see a transaction on the chain's explorer; that is not something we control.

5. How long we keep it

Account, balance and game records are kept while your account exists and for as long as we need them to run the service and meet our obligations. Swap and payment records are kept as transaction history. The IP-to-country cache expires after 30 days. Support messages are kept until resolved and then for a reasonable period for follow-up.

6. Your choices

  • You can stop using NexVault at any time; connecting a wallet and using Telegram are both under your control.
  • You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account data. Balances and open entries will be settled or forfeited as the terms describe before deletion. Records of completed blockchain transactions and payments may be kept where we need them for accounting or legal reasons.
  • To ask, use the Support tab in the app or message @TheNexVaultBot.

7. Security

Data is stored with row-level access controls so one user cannot read another's records, and the app only ever reads your own. Signing happens in your wallet; the site never receives your keys.

8. Children

NexVault is not for anyone under the age of majority where they live. If you believe a minor is using it, contact us and we will remove the account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
