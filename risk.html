Risk Notice

Last updated 31 October 2026 · Please read before you swap or play

Cryptocurrency can lose value quickly and completely. Only use money you can afford to lose. Nothing on NexVault is advice, and no outcome is guaranteed.

Swaps

  • Prices move. The amount you receive is fixed by the blockchain when the transaction settles, not when you see the quote. It can be less than quoted.
  • Transactions are final. Once you confirm in your wallet, no one — not NexVault, not the bridge, not the exchange — can reverse it.
  • Wrong address, lost funds. Tokens sent to a mistyped address, or to an address on the wrong chain, are gone. Paste, then check.
  • Bridges carry extra risk. A cross-chain swap passes through a third-party bridge. Bridges can be slow, can pause, and have been hacked in the past. Large cross-chain swaps can take minutes to hours to arrive.
  • Gas is separate. Your wallet must hold enough of the chain's native coin to pay network fees, on top of the amount you swap. Some wallets reserve more than the fee they display.
  • Tokens can be worthless or fake. Search results list tokens by their on-chain data; a listing is not an endorsement. Scam tokens exist on every chain.
  • Minimums apply. Bridges set their own minimum amounts, which can be high for some routes and change without notice.

Games and NV

  • Entries are not refundable. You pay when you enter; whether you win depends on your play against other players.
  • Not all NV can be withdrawn. Earned and free NV are for playing only. Winnings are cashable only in the proportion your entry was paid with purchased NV. Cash-outs have a minimum and may be queued.
  • Rules can change between rounds: prices, rakes, rewards and tier benefits. The app shows the current rules before each entry.
  • Games may be unavailable where you live. You are responsible for whether playing is lawful for you.

Your wallet and device

  • Your seed phrase is the only thing that controls your funds. NexVault never asks for it. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • Deep links open your wallet app. Always check the amount and address inside the wallet before approving.
  • A compromised phone or Telegram account can be used to sign from your wallet.

Service

  • NexVault depends on blockchains, bridges, wallets, price feeds and Telegram run by others. Any of them can fail or change, and a failure can leave a transaction pending.
  • NexVault is non-custodial and cannot recover, freeze or return funds.

If something looks wrong, stop before confirming, and use the Support tab. Describe what you saw and include the transaction hash if there is one.
